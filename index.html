The afterlife of a loss spike: how Adam's memory writes lasting damage, and how to undo it.

When an LLM's loss spikes, every standard fix (clip, skip, reset, roll back) treats the optimizer as a black box. Optimizer Autopsy measures how much of a spike's lasting damage is carried by the weights versus Adam's m and v, and how fast it migrates between them. It then finds the coordinates that carry it and repairs only those. Every claim is checked against a bit-identical counterfactual replay.

▶ Status, honestly

The fork-and-replay instrument (C1) is built. Its noop-vs-noop Δ == 0 gate holds on CUDA and, since 2026-10-27, on the AMD MI300X, in-process and across processes, through spike windows. The first AMD pilot was exploratory. Width-1 spikes left 0.0003–0.0117 nats of lasting damage, about the size of the 1-ulp chaos floor (≤ 0.003). In the one clear case, undoing m+v removed ~98% of it, and undoing v alone made it worse (n = 1). The next run measures that chaos floor and raises the spike dose until damage clears it. Every other number on this page is still a prediction or a pre-registered proposal, not a result. Full plan: PLAN_V8.md.

00 · The paper in one page

Three contributions, measurement first, plus a released benchmark.

Headline to test: most of a loss spike's lasting damage is written after the spike step, by Adam's memory of it. On a large spike-dominated coordinate the momentum echo writes a fraction β1/√β2 ≈ 92% of the total drift (a corollary of Patitucci & Mokhtari 2026). The inflated v stalls ordinary learning, but it also keeps the echo bounded. So the sharp prediction is that cleaning v alone is worse than doing nothing. We measure the real shares by undoing the spike's contribution to m, v or both at increasing lags. Working title: "Most of a Loss Spike's Damage Is Done After the Spike: A Causal Autopsy of Adam's Memory." We claim the finding and the instrument. We claim neither determinism nor a root cause: the claim is that damage is carried in optimizer state.

C1 · MEASURE

Damage-migration curve

A 2³ (and 2⁴, adding Adam's step counter) cross-world factorial over (w, m, v) at cut times τ ∈ {1, 10, 30, 60, 150, 400}. A β1 × β2 cross identifies which memory carries the damage, and a poisoned-state transplant measures a dose-response. Placebo replicates (1-ulp) bound chaotic sensitivity.

C2 · LOCALIZE

Signed, support-limited, causal

A group-testing oracle finds the minimal state support whose repair recovers the run. Cheap signed predictors are scored against it: attribution patching over (m, v), two-sided innovation tests, Bai's λ_grad by group, and a jump-robust "shadow v". Over- vs under-preconditioning is tracked as a sign.

C3 · REPAIR

Undo it, certify it

A state-only retroactive deletion removes the spike window's direct contribution from m and v; the weights are untouched. A stability-certified restoration makes the smallest log-space move of v on the localized support that puts λ_grad(Ĥ) under (1−δ)·2/η. Scored by gap closed against the best non-oracle baseline.

P3 · RELEASE

SpikeState + toolkit

≥ 200 spike instances with (w, m, v, v̄) snapshots, clean twins, oracle damage maps and a leaderboard. Plus the counterfactual fork engine, an undo-log optimizer wrapper and an optimizer-state integrity scanner.

SCALE

Does it transfer?

Three levels. L0: β-set rates hold at any scale. L1: amplitudes should collapse under μP. L2: finite-size effects are measured. A scaling law is fitted on small configs and must predict held-out rungs before they run. Real trunks are borrowed from released Pythia and OPEN-1B optimizer states.

⚠ Route, don't kill

The gates decide what runs first. They never delete an idea. A null state-share becomes the "weight-carried" paper. If a simple band-clamp matches our repair, it becomes the practical recommendation. If ROCm can't reach Δ = 0, the fork-free detector and online undo lead.

01 · Method

Fork, cut, hybridize, localize, repair, replay.

Every branch (ours and every baseline) forks from the same snapshot, so two trajectories differ only by the edit under test. The clean counterfactual B* keeps the data stream aligned. The effect is always Δ = val_loss(branch) − val_loss(B*) on held-out batches that are disjoint from every probe.

03 · The math that makes predictions

Closed forms we will test, not assume.

These are derivations [I], checked by hand. Each becomes a pre-registered test. Defaults are (β1, β2) = (0.9, 0.95).

Damage and its decomposition
Δ_X(h) = L_val(X, t1+h) − L_val(B*, t1+h) # held-out, end of schedule Θ^{abc} = (w^a, m^b, v^c, k), a,b,c ∈ {B0, B*} # 2³ cross-world hybrids φ_state = ½[D(011) + D(111) − D(100)] # Shapley share of (m,v)
Honest-Adam feasibility (every real state satisfies it)
|m_i| ≤ C·√v_i, C = (1−β1) / √(1−β2) / √(1 − β1²/β2) = 1.165
Anti-windup invariance: why clipping only softens spikes
update/η on a spike-dominated coordinate = (1−β1)/√(1−β2) · (β1/√β2)^k = 0.447 · 0.9234^k total drift → D = (1−β1) / [√(1−β2) · (1 − β1/√β2)] · η = 5.84η · 10.47η · 31.8η (β2 = .95 · .99 · .999) # a saturating limit as G/ḡ → ∞: clipping a large spike barely changes D (smaller spikes drift less)
Which memory carries it? (the identification problem)
v excess decays as β2^τ → half-life 13.5 steps at β2 = 0.95 closed-loop (w, m) modes decay at √β1 → half-life 13.2 steps at β1 = 0.9 # 2.7% apart # so V8 crosses β1 × β2: (.9,.95) (.9,.99) (.9,.999) (.8,.95) (.95,.95)
Planted-poison recovery law
k½ = ln((κ−1)/3) / (−ln β2) ≈ 158 steps (κ = 10⁴, β2 = 0.95)
Repairs
undo: m −= Σ_{s∈W} (1−β1) β1^{t1−s} g_s v −= Σ_{s∈W} (1−β2) β2^{t1−s} (g_s² − v̄), v ≥ 0 # weights untouched; no ÷β error growth restore: v_S(α) = v_S^{1−α} · T_S^α, largest α with λ_grad(Ĥ) ≤ (1−δ)·2/η # Bai's Ĥ (threshold 2/η); raw D⁻¹H is 38/η

06 · Idea portfolio

About 140 ideas, all kept and labelled honestly.

The full table with verdicts and tiers is in PLAN V8 Appendix A. They come from two research waves that borrowed from causal inference, estimation theory, finance, data assimilation, reliability engineering, security and scaling theory. A few highlights:

MECHANISM

Protection law

A spike's v-excess may protect its own damage from being overwritten. Restoration works by removing that protection (after Biswas 2026). It predicts which spikes self-heal.

ESTIMATION

Shadow v

A bipower-variation copy of v (from finance) that a single jump barely moves. Its jump share localizes with zero forks and zero logs, and fails by design at width ≥ 2.

RELIABILITY

Bit-flip map

A flip that inflates v is silent: the coordinate freezes with no loss spike, permanently if v becomes ∞. A flip that shrinks v, or any flip in m, spikes at once. Undo fixes gradient faults; a parity residual catches state faults.

FORENSICS

Recover the offending tokens

Δlog v on embedding rows between two snapshots may reveal which tokens caused the spike, with no data logs. It is also a privacy note for anyone releasing optimizer states.

SCALE

A law that predicts

Fit on five small configs, then predict the mid rung and 124M before running them. Under μP the amplitudes should collapse; under SP the exponent is the finding.

SECURITY

Spikes as attacks on state

v too large freezes, v too small destabilizes, a poisoned m steers. An integrity scanner checks shipped optimizer.pt files against honest-Adam invariants.

07 · Pre-attacked

What could sink this, and where each risk routes.

Marin 32B says the opposite: a direct counter-case

Marin's retrospective inferred, by elimination, that its spikes were "rooted in the model's attention stack rather than in optimizer state". An optimizer-state rebuild relapsed. Route: our factorial measures exactly this split, and the QK-Norm arm reports it either way. We claim "carried in", never "caused by".

ROCm may not reach bitwise Δ = 0

On ROCm, use_deterministic_algorithms doesn't reach hipBLASLt, and torch 2.10's AOTriton attention backward ignores the deterministic flag. Route: env vars in the launcher, math-backend attention, torch 2.13 if needed, then the pre-registered tolerance. If all of those fail, the fork-free detector and online undo lead.

The state share could be ≈ 0 after ~50 steps

At β2 = 0.95, v forgets in about 100 steps. Route: that is H2's prediction, and the result is "weight-carried after k steps", with the undo log as the in-window remedy.

A dumb clamp might match the clever repair

If v ← clip(v, a·v̄, b·v̄) matches certified restoration, localization adds nothing. Route: it is a required baseline. If it wins, it becomes the practical recommendation and localization becomes analysis.

"A 3-layer toy at step 160"

Route: a realistic proxy, a mid rung, 124M n = 5, borrowed Pythia and OPEN-1B states, and a scaling law that predicts held-out rungs. Molybog's small-scale caveat is quoted and answered, not hidden.

The undo log isn't new

Correct for the mechanism: SWIFT (TPDS 2024) inverts Adam one step back. Route: it is cited as the ancestor. Ours is the retroactive window, state only, the forward form with no error growth, and the direct/indirect path split.
